## 2025-11-12

🇱🇰 #SriLanka #Census 2024
🆕 Data Release!

I’m converting the 2024 Census of Population and Housing into clean, structured, and fully machine-readable datasets — openly available on @GitHub.

<https://github.com/nuuuwan/lk_census_2024￼>

📊 10 datasets so far, covering #population, #housing, #ethnicity, #religion, #age, #water, #lighting, and more.
📁 JSON & TSV, with links to the original source PDFs.
🌐 Extracted and validated from <http://statistics.gov.lk>.

-- #OpenData #CPH2024

## 2026-03-24

🇱🇰 #SriLanka #Census 2024
🆕 Data Update

Added 8 new tables from the official XLSX files, covering Grama Niladhari Division (GND) level data — ~14,000 GNDs, aggregated up to DSD, District, Province, and Country.

<https://github.com/nuuuwan/lk_census_2024>

📊 18 datasets now, covering #population by sex & age, #housing units, #households, #water, #energy, #lighting, and #sanitation.
📁 JSON & TSV, with links to the original source XLSXs and PDFs.
🌐 Extracted and validated from <http://statistics.gov.lk>.

-- #OpenData #CPH2024 #GND
